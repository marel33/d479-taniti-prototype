Transportation

Plan your arrival and how you will get around Taniti.

After You Land: Most visitors arrive through Taniti's small airport. A rental-car agency is near the airport. Taxis are available in Taniti City, and public buses operate in Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. This prototype does not confirm direct bus or taxi service from the airport, so arrange the connection to your lodging in advance.

Travel Info & FAQ

Quick answers for currency, power, safety, healthcare, holidays, and other practical travel questions.

What currency is used?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen.

How can I pay?

The U.S. dollar is the primary currency. Euros and yen may also be accepted, and major credit cards are commonly accepted.

What power outlets are used?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Is Taniti safe?

Violent crime is very rare, though visitors should watch for petty crimes such as pickpocketing as tourism increases.

Is healthcare available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

What languages are spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Less English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Are there alcohol restrictions?

Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m. The drinking age is 18.

Do holidays affect hours?

Taniti has many national holidays, and some attractions and restaurants may close on those days.
